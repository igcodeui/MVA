MVA INTAKE

Motor Vehicle Accident Engine

Interface Workspace:
⚖️

Compliance Reminder: You are not an attorney. Do not give legal advice, mention deadlines, statutes of limitations, or imply any outcome.

Notes: Take detailed notes on every answer. Use the client's exact words. Do not evaluate or interpret.

Introduction — Identify Yourself
1A — Inbound (They Call You)

Agent: Hey, this is (Agent Name) with Car Accident Payout — how can I assist you today?

Agent: Great! Let's start off with this — can I just confirm the state you're calling from, your first and last name, and your phone number?

1B — Outbound (You Call Them)

Agent: Hi (Client Name), this is (Your Name) calling from Car Accident Payout — can you hear me okay?

Agent: I'm following up because you recently reached out after a vehicle accident. Is that right?

Step 1 — Setting Expectations

Agent: Great! I just have a few short questions to make sure I have all the info I need — then, if you would like, I can get you in contact with a licensed attorney. Does that work for you?

🚨 STOP: Get a clear Yes before continuing. If hesitant or no, end the call politely.

Step 2 — Location & Accident Timing

Agent: What state did the accident take place in?

Agent: Do you remember the date the accident happened?

-- Note: If unsure of exact date, ask roughly when (e.g. End of November 2024).

Statute of Limitations (SOL) Matrix Rule:

• KY & TN: 12 months max SOL (so no older than 6 months).
• All other states: ~24 months SOL (so no older than 1.5 years).

Step 3 — Incident & Case Details

Agent: Can you quickly describe what happened? Did they hit you from the driver side or passenger side?

Agent: Was this your personal car, or did it involve a commercial vehicle — like a truck, delivery van, or company car?

Collision Type — confirm and note one:

Head-On: Two vehicles hit front-to-front.
Rear-End: One vehicle crashes into the back of another.
T-Bone: One vehicle strikes the side of another. Any angle — if hit on the side, it is a T-bone.
Multi-Vehicle: Three or more vehicles in a chain reaction.
Other: Everything else, or if client is unsure.

Agent: Were you the driver or a passenger?

If DRIVER: Ask: Any passengers? How many? Any injured? Do they need representation? Note names/contact if possible.

If PASSENGER: Ask: Who was driving? Were they injured? Do they need representation? Get their contact at the end.

Step 4 — Fault

Agent: Who was at fault for the accident?

ℹ️ EDGE CASE: Fault is disputed BUT there are significant injuries or a fatality — continue.
Step 5 — Legal Status & Settlement A. Attorney Representation

Agent: Are you currently represented by an attorney for this accident, or have you previously worked with one for this same incident?

Step 6 — Injuries

Agent: Were you physically injured in the accident?

Follow-Up Questions

Agent: I'm sorry to hear that. Can you describe the injuries briefly?

Agent: What side were you injured?

Agent: How would you rate that pain on a scale from 1 to 10? 1 is mild, 10 is the worst pain imaginable.

-- Note: Use their exact words. Do not evaluate or interpret.

Injury Reference • Soft tissue: Whiplash, sprains, bruising, scratches.
• Medium: Herniated discs, torn ligaments, concussions.
• Significant: Fractures, spinal injuries, internal / organ damage.
Step 7 — Initial Treatment

Agent: Did you see a doctor, go to urgent care, or visit the ER within 30 days after the accident?

Agent: Did you have surgery — or do you think you might need it?

-- Note: Note the surgery answer. It does not disqualify either way.

ℹ️ EDGE CASE: Signing up >30 days post-accident: client must show continuous treatment with no gaps exceeding 30 days — OR have a significant documented injury. Verify at Step 8.
Step 8 — Ongoing Treatment

Agent: Are you still receiving medical treatment? When is your next appointment?

-- Note: If yes — ask how often. Note any gaps.

⚠️ EDGE CASE: If client is vague or inconsistent about injuries or treatment — make them repeat slowly. Uncertainty is a red flag.
Step 9 — Police Report

Agent: Was a police report filed? Do you have a copy or a case number?

• Nice to have — not required to qualify.

• If they have a copy/number, ask them to send it via SMS or email.

• -- Hit & Run: the at-fault driver's insurance (if any) will appear on the police report.

📌 RULE: The case requires either valid at-fault insurance, OR confirmed UM/UIM coverage. Ask both before deciding.
A. At-Fault Driver Insurance

Agent: Do you know if the other driver had car insurance at the time of the accident?

-- Note: insured / uninsured / hit-and-run / unknown.

B. Client UM/UIM Coverage

Agent: Do you have your own car insurance? Is it liability only, or full coverage?

Agent: Do you have Uninsured or Underinsured Motorist coverage — sometimes called UM or UIM — on your policy?

-- Note: Full coverage includes UM/UIM automatically. Liability-only or unknown — confirm explicitly.

Closing & Retainer

Agent: Thank you (sir/ma'am) — I've got everything I need. I'm just going to quickly prepare the agreement. The agreement allows the attorney to review your case and reach out to you directly.

Agent: Before I send it over, could I get your date of birth, current street address, and city? And would you prefer it through email or SMS?

-- Note: Prepare doc and send via their preferred method.

Walk Them Through Signing

Agent: You should see it come through into your (email / SMS) — do you see it? Please open it and let me know when it's in front of you. Agent: At the top, there's a prompt to agree to the terms and conditions — please click agree and continue. Agent: Scroll down a little — you should see a green box to sign. Click it. A small window will open with fields for your name, initials, and signature. Agent: At the bottom-left of that window, check the box that says fill out in all fields, then click OK at the bottom-right. Agent: Now at the very top of the document, click Finish.

⚠️ Confirm in ZOHO Sign that the document is completed before moving on.

Transfer

Agent: Thank you so much (Client Name) — let me try to get you connected with the firm now.

-- Note: Attempt transfer.

If firm picks up:

Agent: Hi there, this is (Your Name) with Car Accident Payout. I have (Client Name) on the line — they just signed your retainer. Can you please confirm you received it?

-- Confirm name, number, and email with the rep.

Agent: Thank you so much — have a great day. - end

Disposition — Disqualified Lead

Agent: Ok, I understand. Based on what you've shared, this particular program may not be the right fit for you — but that doesn't mean you're without options.

Agent: You're always welcome to get a second opinion elsewhere. Take care.

🚨 DO NOT — explain why they were not transferred. Do not suggest legal alternatives. Do not imply case strength or weakness. End professionally and immediately.
