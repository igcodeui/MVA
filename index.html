Step 1 — Setting Expectations Verification

“Great! I just have a few short questions to make sure I have all the info I need — then, if you would like, I can get you in contact with a licensed attorney. Does that work for you?”

🚨 STOP — Get a clear "Yes" before continuing. If hesitant or no, end the call politely.

Step 2 — Location & Accident Timing SOL Check

“What state did the accident take place in?”
“Do you remember the date the accident happened?”

Note: If unsure of exact date, ask roughly when (e.g. "End of November 2024").

Statute of Limitations (SOL) Matrix Rule:

• KY & TN: 12 months max SOL (so no older than 6 months).
• All other states: ~24 months SOL (so no older than 1.5 years).

Step 3 — Incident & Case Details Details

“Can you quickly describe what happened? Did they hit you from the driver side or passenger side?”

“Was this your personal car, or did it involve a commercial vehicle — like a truck, delivery van, or company car?”

Verify & Classify Crash Logic:

Head-On: Two vehicles hit front-to-front.
Rear-End: One vehicle crashes into the back of another.
T-Bone: One vehicle strikes the side of another. Any angle — if hit on the side, it is a T-bone.
Multi-Vehicle: Three or more vehicles in a chain reaction.
Other: Everything else, or if client is unsure.

“Were you the driver or a passenger?”

• If DRIVER: Ask: Any passengers? How many? Any injured? Do they need representation? Note names/contact.

• If PASSENGER: Ask: Who was driving? Were they injured? Do they need representation?

Step 4 — Fault Evaluation Liability

“Who was at fault for the accident?”

⚠️ Edge Case Rule: If fault is disputed BUT there are significant injuries or a fatality — select CONTINUE path.
Step 5 — Legal Status & Settlement Exclusivity

“Are you currently represented by an attorney for this accident, or have you previously worked with one for this same incident?”

Step 6 — Injury Assessment Medical

“Were you physically injured in the accident?”

“I'm sorry to hear that. Can you describe the injuries briefly?”

“What side were you injured?”

“How would you rate that pain on a scale from 1 to 10? 1 is mild, 10 is the worst pain imaginable.”

⚠️ Injury Severity Reference Guide:
• Soft tissue: Whiplash, sprains, bruising, scratches.
• Medium: Herniated discs, torn ligaments, concussions.
• Significant: Fractures, spinal injuries, internal organ damage.
Step 7 — Initial Treatment Timeline

“Did you see a doctor, go to urgent care, or visit the ER within 30 days after the accident?”

“Did you have surgery — or do you think you might need it?”

⚠️ Edge Case Rule: If treatment started >30 days post-accident, client must prove continuous treatment with no gaps exceeding 30 days — OR possess a significant documented injury (fracture, head trauma).
Step 8 — Ongoing Treatment Gaps

“Are you still receiving medical treatment? When is your next appointment?”

Note: If yes ask how often. Note any gaps.

⚠️ Disqualification Marker: Soft tissue injury with an unexcused treatment gap of 2-3 months or more disqualifies the lead. Uncertainty is a red flag.
Step 9 — Police Report Nice to Have

“Was a police report filed? Do you have a copy or a case number?”

• Nice to have — not required to qualify. If they have a copy or number, ask them to send it via SMS/email. Note details.
Step 10 — Insurance Coverage Matrix Mandatory Path
📌 Coverage Mandate Rule: Either the at-fault driver had valid insurance, OR the client has confirmed UM/UIM coverage. Ask both questions before deciding.

“Do you know if the other driver had car insurance at the time of the accident?”

“Do you have your own car insurance? Is it liability only, or full coverage?”

“Do you have Uninsured or Underinsured Motorist coverage — sometimes called UM or UIM — on your policy?”

Disqualified Lead Readout

“Ok, I understand. Based on what you've shared, this particular program may not be the right fit for you — but that doesn't mean you're without options.”

“You're always welcome to get a second opinion elsewhere. Take care.”

🚨 CRITICAL DIRECTIVE: DO NOT explain why they were not transferred. Do not suggest legal alternatives. Do not imply case strength or weakness. End professionally and immediately.

⚠️ Escalation Signals

Pause conversation and dispo immediately if lead asks for legal advice, requests opt-out, appears to be a minor, or states they are represented.
