⚠️ Stop & Escalation Triggers

Pause conversation and notify compliance supervisor immediately if:

  • Lead asks for legal advice / case valuation
  • Lead requests opt-out in any form
  • Lead appears to be a minor
  • Lead expresses distress or threatens legal action
  • Consent documentation missing/unclear
  • Lead's state cannot be identified (Tier 3 rules)
Objection Handling Library
“Do I have a case?” / “Am I going to win?”

“That’s an important question, and I want to make sure you get the right answer. Our team is not able to evaluate the outcome of your situation — we’re an intake service, not a law firm. A licensed attorney would be best positioned to review the specifics. I can help connect you with one if you’d like.”

“How much will I get?” / “What’s my case worth?”

“Settlement amounts depend on many factors specific to your situation, and we’re not able to evaluate that. A licensed attorney would be better positioned to discuss it with you.”

“Is this free?” / “What does it cost?”

“The review itself is handled directly by the attorney — this step simply allows them to look over your situation and get in touch. Fee structures vary by firm, so that’s a conversation to have directly with them.”

“Take me off your list” / “Stop contacting me”

“Absolutely, I completely understand. I’ve noted your request and will ensure your information is removed from our outreach list immediately. You will not receive any further messages from us.”

[Document: date, time, agent ID, lead ID. Suppress in CRM immediately.]
“Are you my lawyer?” / “Do you represent me?”

“No — we’re an intake service, not a law firm. Representation would be established directly between you and any attorney you choose to work with. We can help connect you with one.”

“What’s the statute of limitations?” / “How long do I have to file?”

“That’s a legal question we’re not qualified to answer. Time limits vary by state and situation — a licensed attorney in your state would be able to advise you on any deadlines that may apply.”

“Who gave you my number?” / “How did you get this?”

“That’s a fair question. Your information was provided through [lead source / form you submitted on (date if known)]. If you’d prefer not to be contacted further, I can remove you from our list right now — just say the word.”

“This is required, right?” / “Do I have to do this?”

“Not at all — I want to be transparent. There’s no legal requirement for you to respond to our outreach. Proceeding is entirely your choice, and there’s no obligation.”

Lead is upset, hostile, or threatens legal action

“I’m sorry for the frustration — that’s the last thing we want. Let me remove you from our list right now and escalate this to my supervisor. You won’t hear from us again.”

[End contact. Suppress immediately. Notify compliance supervisor.]
